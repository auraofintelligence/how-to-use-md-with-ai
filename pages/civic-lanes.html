Choose what stays private and what helps the public.

A Markdown file can help artificial intelligence (AI) understand your intent, respect your privacy, and prepare clearer public records. The aim is simple: people keep control of their own story, and public power leaves a trail people can follow.

Plain words first

These ideas sound technical until they become everyday choices: write the private note, share the approved summary, publish the notice, keep the receipt.

.md

Markdown file

A plain text note with headings. It is easy for people to read and easy for AI tools to follow.

AI

Artificial intelligence

A computer tool that can draft, sort, summarise or compare text when you give it useful context.

Twin

Digital twin

A helpful digital record of a person, place, project or community asset. It can start as a folder of careful notes.

Aura

Personal support layer

The private layer around a person: preferences, needs, goals, boundaries, reminders and care notes.

Avatar

Public or game-world self

The version of a person, project or group that appears on a website, in a public profile or inside a game world.

Self

Self-sovereign control

The person or community stays in charge of their own record, permissions, corrections and sharing choices.

Open public-accountability sources

A North Stradbroke Island example

A local group wants to organise a working bee, invite volunteers and share an update afterwards. The private file keeps organiser phone numbers, health needs and internal notes. The trusted file helps the small team prepare the day. The public notice tells locals what is happening, where to go and who has approved the message. The receipt records what was promised, what happened and what still needs follow-up.

That is the heart of the system. The AI can help draft, shorten, translate, check sources and prepare versions for a wall screen or phone post. People keep the final say.

Private aura.md

Needs, boundaries, helpers, risks and details that stay with the organiser.

Trusted team_brief.md

Run sheet, equipment, volunteer roles and questions for the small group.

Public notice.md

Approved plain-English message for the community noticeboard.

Receipt outcome.md

Dates, decisions, sources, thanks, corrections and next steps.

From private aura to public accountability

The wider ecosystem can grow from ordinary files. A personal aura becomes a controlled support layer. A public avatar becomes an approved doorway. A noticeboard becomes a shared memory. A game-world quest becomes a useful civic action. A receipt trail becomes public accountability.

P4A-XYZ in plain English

P4A-XYZ is a working name for a grassroots governance model. It asks communities to protect private life while making public power easier to inspect. The braided economy is the same idea applied to value: care, volunteering, skills, culture, local assets and public money can be recorded in different ways, with the right privacy level for each one.
