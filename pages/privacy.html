Privacy notes.

This site is designed as a static public guide with a browser-local builder. It helps you create a file. It does not need to collect the file.

What happens in the builder

Your form answers are stored in this browser's `localStorage` so the preview can keep working if you move around the page. The generated Markdown is created by JavaScript in the browser. Downloading creates a file on your device. Copying places the text on your clipboard.

What this site does not do

The site does not include a database, login, analytics package, contact form, server-side storage or GitHub write action. It does not publish your Markdown file. You control whether you copy, download, edit, share or delete it.

Be cautious with private details and records

This site does not collect your file or your form answers. The risk starts when you paste details into an artificial intelligence tool, upload them to another service, save them on a shared computer, or a scammer gets access to your device. Private records can be used to rip you off, blackmail you or steal from you. Most of us already have details scattered across the internet, so privacy is not automatic.

If you understand the terms and conditions of the artificial intelligence service or computer service you are using, and you are comfortable with the risk, choose what details you want to use. The point is informed choice, not pretending a website can protect everything for you.

Before using a file with an artificial intelligence tool

Read the generated file first and decide what is private, public, trusted-only or still a draft. If the file includes details about other people, use consent and care before sharing it outside your own device.
